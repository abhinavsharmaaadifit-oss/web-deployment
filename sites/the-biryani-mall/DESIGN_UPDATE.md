# Website preview design refresh

This update applies a consistent premium page structure inspired by the The Biryani Mall homepage while retaining each prospect's original business-specific content, palette, imagery references, contact data, ratings, review counts, address, hours, and Maps search links.

Each website now includes: a complete sticky header and accessible mobile navigation; a spacious two-column hero; a quick-information strip; better-spaced service, story and gallery sections; a contact panel with a Google Maps embed and external directions link; a multi-column footer with navigation and contact information; and a mobile call/directions action bar.

All business photos remain illustrative concept imagery and should be replaced with owner-approved photos before a final production launch. Public business details and operating hours should be reconfirmed with the owner. The preview disclaimer remains visible.
