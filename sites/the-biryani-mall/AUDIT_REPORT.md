## Important scope and limitations

- This package contains 10 proposed static website concepts. They are previews only, not official websites and not a claim that the business has approved the content.
- Google Maps fields below are a lookup snapshot, not a permanent guarantee. Ratings, review counts, hours and listings can change.
- The Maps links open a Google Maps search for the matched business name and address. They are included for manual pin confirmation, not claimed to be verified share links to a canonical Place URL.
- Phone numbers are drawn from the master sheet and checked against the business lookup/public listings where possible. They have not been call-tested.
- No prices, offers, staff names, owner names, booking integrations or payment features have been invented. Service categories are based on public listings and are still subject to owner approval.
- Images in the previews are representative stock/concept imagery, not verified photos of the businesses. Replace with owner-approved images before any public launch.
- No online ordering, payment, booking engine, lead storage or back-end form submission is active. The functional actions are call and Google Maps directions only.

## Build notes

Each website lives at `sites/<slug>/index.html`. Every HTML file includes its own CSS and JavaScript, so each folder can be uploaded independently to a static host or to the matching folder in the central GitHub repository.

All ten pages include:
- Responsive desktop, tablet and mobile layouts
- Mobile navigation and a fixed mobile call/directions bar
- Business-specific colour theme and copy
- Click-to-call phone link
- Google Maps search/directions link
- LocalBusiness-style JSON-LD with the public business name, phone and address
- A visible concept-preview notice and image disclaimer

## Owner confirmation checklist before approval / live publication

1. Confirm exact legal/trading name and preferred logo.
2. Confirm exact Google Business Profile pin, complete postal address and branch.
3. Confirm the phone number and WhatsApp number separately.
4. Confirm current services, menu/course details, prices and opening hours.
5. Supply approved logo, real venue/team/product photos, and social media links.
6. Confirm website/domain ownership and whether a website already exists.
7. Approve the final copy and privacy/contact requirements before adding any enquiry form, booking or payment integration.
